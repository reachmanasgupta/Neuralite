## 🌟 About the Project
Neuralite is a comprehensive, futuristic, and full-stack AI-powered healthcare platform built to bridge the gap between advanced machine learning and accessible medical care. Designed with a modern, high-end medical-tech UI/UX.

---

## ✨ Key Features (9 Smart AI Modules)

1. **🤖 Neuralite AI Chatbot:** Voice-enabled smart symptom checker with multilingual support.
2. **📄 AI Parchi (Prescription) Scanner:** Digitizes handwritten prescriptions and extracts medicines.
3. **🩻 Pneumonia X-Ray Scanner:** Deep Learning (CNN) based chest X-Ray analysis.
4. **📊 Health Risk Predictor:** ML-based tabular data analysis for overall health risk scoring.
5. **🥗 Wellness & Diet Coach:** Personalized lifestyle and Indian diet plan recommendations.
6. **🧠 Mental Health Analyzer:** Stress and psychological assessment based on standard inputs.
7. **🔬 Skin / Dermatology Analyzer:** AI-powered visual analysis for skin rashes and conditions.
8. **📋 Lab Report Analyzer:** Reads blood test reports and explains abnormal values.
9. **🍎 Food Calorie Scanner:** Scans meal photos to estimate calories, proteins, and health scores.

---

## 🚀 Core Platform Features
* **Live Video Consultations:** Integrated with ZegoCloud for seamless 1-on-1 doctor-patient video calls.
* **Secure Payments:** Razorpay integration for online doctor appointment fees and health services.
* **E-Prescription & Vitals:** Real-time health vitals tracking and digital prescription vaults.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Recharts, Lucide Icons
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Authentication & Appointments)
* **ML Backend:** Python, Flask, TensorFlow / Keras, Google Gemini AI APIs
